1. **No competitor owns agency-to-agency (white-label/overflow) content.** All eight benchmarked agencies write for direct brand clients only. This is the single clearest white-space opportunity and is acted on directly in Cluster D (agency-vertical subtopics) and the Signature Asset (Section 6).

## 3. Target Audience & Buyer Personas

**Every content brief in this plan must declare which of the three personas below it is written for — no brief may target a generic "business owner."**

### Primary Persona — "The Accountable Marketing Manager" *(derived, brand-side; core economic-influence persona for the bulk of pillar/cluster traffic — reflects the CRO framework's resolved primary reader)*
- **Role:** In-house Marketing Manager or Marketing Coordinator at a mid-market Melbourne/Australian business (trades, hospitality, professional services, retail, education/childcare, fitness — matches the existing pillar's industries grid).
- **Company size:** Typically 10–150 staff; marketing is a team of 1–4, often just this person plus outsourced specialists.
- **Geography:** Melbourne-metro primary, Victoria and national secondary (matches Geo Mode: MULTI-LOCATION).
- **Current state:** Running social media themselves or via a generalist agency; can report reach and follower growth but cannot confidently report cost-per-lead or ROI to leadership.
- **Content consumption habits:** Passive discovery via Google search during "is this normal / what should this cost" moments; active reading of how-to and benchmark content on lunch breaks or end-of-day; downloads checklists and templates; low tolerance for jargon-heavy long reads.
- **Core pain point (in their words):** *"I can't tell my boss what social actually returned last quarter."*
- **Decision trigger:** A leadership meeting or board report where social performance is questioned directly.
- **Preferred format:** Short, benchmark-driven blog posts with a clear number in the title; downloadable audit/checklist tools; case studies with one hero metric, not a wall of stats.

### Secondary Persona — Rachel Nguyen, "The Overstretched Agency Ops Lead" *(as supplied in full ICP; internal champion who influences but does not hold final sign-off)*
- **Role:** Head of Client Services at a ~95-staff Melbourne digital marketing agency (Richmond).
- **Current state:** Social media run as an under-resourced "value-add," two junior coordinators (one just resigned), account managers absorbing overflow work off-scope.
- **Content consumption habits:** Scattered, problem-framed search ("why is client social engagement dropping," "how to reduce agency staff turnover") rather than solution-category search; peer conversations at Melbourne agency meetups and LinkedIn groups; occasional guide/checklist downloads that speak to "agency running an agency" pain, not generic SMB social advice.
- **Core pain point (in her words):** *"Every time someone leaves, we're back to square one."*
- **Decision trigger:** A client formally raises social performance as a retainer-review reason, or a second team resignation makes the capacity gap undeniable.
- **Preferred format:** Operationally-framed long-form guides and a TOFU lead magnet that names her exact symptoms without yet pitching a solution category (per her own Section 13 recommendation: *"Why Agencies Struggle to Keep Social Media Consistent"*).

### Tertiary Persona — "The Competitor-Frustrated Switcher" *(derived — actively re-evaluating providers)*
- **Role:** Marketing Manager or small-business owner currently paying an existing social media agency or freelancer and dissatisfied with vanity-metric reporting, inconsistent posting, or lack of ROI clarity.
- **Current state:** Mid-contract or just past a minimum term with an incumbent provider; has specific, comparative questions rather than educational ones.
- **Content consumption habits:** Active, late-stage search behaviour — comparison and "vs" style queries, review sites, direct competitor-page visits; reads pricing and comparison content specifically; skips top-of-funnel educational material.
- **Core pain point (in their words):** *"Our current agency just sends us a screenshot of likes every month."*
- **Decision trigger:** Contract renewal date approaching, or a specific reporting failure (can't answer "what did this cost per lead").
- **Preferred format:** Comparison tables, transparent pricing-structure content, decision-criteria checklists, and case studies with named, dated, verifiable outcomes.

## 8. Distribution & Amplification Plan

*Only channels confirmed in Distribution Channels Available (GBP, email, Instagram, LinkedIn, blog) are included. Paid amplification and PR/backlink outreach infrastructure were not confirmed and are omitted rather than planned for.*

### 8.1 Organic Distribution (Always-On)

- **On-page SEO checklist per article:** target keyword in H1/title tag/first 100 words, meta description within 150–160 characters, one internal link up to the pillar, 2–3 sideways links to sibling cluster content, at least one featured-snippet-formatted FAQ block (per Section 5.3 question bank), image alt text on every visual.
- **Internal linking minimum:** every cluster article — 1 link up, 2–3 links sideways (per interlinking rule, Section 4).
- **Schema markup:** `Article` on every long-form/mid-form piece, `FAQPage` on any article carrying a PAA-targeted question block, `HowTo` on Cluster B process-style articles (content brief, approval workflow, repurposing guide).
- **Search Console monitoring cadence:** weekly check of new-piece indexation status; monthly full-query performance review (ties to Section 9 leading/lagging indicators).

### 8.2 Email Distribution

- **Newsletter cadence:** Fortnightly (matches the 2 long-form + 2 mid-form/week output rate sustainably without over-committing a 1-writer team to a weekly send).
- **Structure:** One featured long-form piece, one mid-form/quick-answer piece, one CTA (audit tool or strategy call, per the CRO framework's four-rung ladder).
- **Nurture sequence:** A 4-email sequence triggered by audit-tool or lead-magnet download, sourced entirely from already-published cluster content — Email 1: relevant Cluster A piece (problem framing), Email 2: relevant Cluster D vertical piece (matched to the lead's stated industry where known), Email 3: relevant Cluster C piece (cost/ROI, addressing the pricing-transparency gap), Email 4: direct strategy-call CTA.
- **Segmentation logic:** Persona × industry vertical — brand-side leads segmented by the seven industries in the existing pillar's industries grid (trades, hospitality, professional services, education/childcare, fitness, retail, construction); agency-partner leads (Rachel-type) segmented separately into the agency-vertical nurture track using Cluster D's two agency-specific pieces.

### 8.3 LinkedIn / Instagram Amplification

- **Company page cadence:** 6 posts/week (matches stated social post capacity), rotating between short-form video repurposes, static/carousel data points from cluster articles, and GBP-style local proof points.
- **Re-share pattern:** every new long-form article re-shared as a LinkedIn native carousel within 48 hours of blog publish; every short-form video cross-posted to Instagram Reels and LinkedIn video.
- **Engagement quota:** Writer allocates time to reply to every comment within 24 hours on TrafficRadius's own posts (modelling the "response-time benchmark" standard the Cluster B article itself teaches).
- **Google Business Profile:** 1 post/week tied to that week's Cluster D vertical or A/C theme, reinforcing local-commercial keyword relevance for the "social media marketing agency melbourne" head term.

### 10.3 Quality Checklist (Pre-Publish)

- **Originality/AI-boilerplate standard:** No generic, template-shaped claims; every piece must include at least one TrafficRadius-specific angle, example, or (where available) proprietary data point — no content ships that could have been published by any of the eight benchmarked competitors verbatim.
- **Accuracy/sourcing standard (ACCC / Australian Consumer Law):** No statistic, benchmark, or performance figure is published without a stated, checkable source. No claim implies a guaranteed outcome (no "guaranteed," no absolute ranking/result promises, consistent with the banned-terms list already established for this client). Any client case-study figure used in content must carry the same `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]` / results-vary discipline already applied to the pillar page.
- **Voice/localisation standard:** Melbourne/Australian spelling and terminology throughout; plain, benchmark-driven language over agency jargon (per the CRO framework's Finding 4.5 — no "leveraging," "holistic," "dominate").
- **Compliance standard:** Every published piece checked against ACCC guidance on misleading or deceptive conduct — no implied guarantee, no unsubstantiated superlative ("leading," "best," "#1") without evidence.
- **Accessibility standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA — descriptive alt text on all images, correct H1→H2→H3 heading hierarchy (no skipped levels), sufficient colour contrast on any custom graphics, descriptive (non "click here") link text.
