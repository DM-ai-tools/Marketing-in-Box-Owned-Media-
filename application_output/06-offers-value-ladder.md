## RUNG 0 — FREE / LEAD GENERATION

**1. Service (Diagnostic Audit): "Free Audit Reveals The 7 Social Media Mistakes Quietly Draining Your Marketing Budget"**
- Description: A self-service intake form feeds a written diagnostic report reviewing current channels, posting cadence and ad spend, flagging exactly where consistency and budget are leaking, plus a prioritised 90-day fix list. Consumption time under 20 minutes.
- Solves: The ICP's stated inability to prove what social is or isn't delivering, and the vague, mounting sense — echoed by client complaints about flatlined engagement — that channels are underperforming without evidence of why.
- Estimated Value: $450 — equivalent to roughly 3 hours of senior strategist diagnostic time at standard agency day rates.
- Price: Free — exchanged for an email address, phone number and a short intake questionnaire.
- Rationale: Meets the buyer where she actually is — problem-aware but not yet searching in solution-category terms (per the ICP's research behaviour) — with zero risk and a named, specific diagnostic rather than a generic "get a quote" form. None of the benchmarked competitors (Spicy Web, Website Designs Agency, Print Video Web, SEO First Page) publish a social-specific free diagnostic; all default to enquiry forms once past the marketing page.
- Ascends To: Rung 1 — the paid Fix-It Kit.
- Delivery Load: Low — templated audit format runs on a standard checklist; ~1–2 analyst hours per lead, sustainable within current agency headcount without adding to the account-manager overflow burden the ICP is trying to eliminate.

## RUNG 1 — LOW-TICKET

**2. Information (Template/Playbook): "Fix Your Social Calendar In 48 Hours — Without Hiring Anyone New"**
- Description: A fixed-price, done-for-you deliverable: a strategist audits the current presence and builds a ready-to-run 30-day content calendar, posting cadence and brand-voice brief, handed over within 48 hours of a short intake call. No ongoing commitment required.
- Solves: "We keep saying we'll sort out social next quarter and it never happens" and inconsistent output when internal capacity is stretched — a fast, tangible fix for a buyer not yet ready to commit to a retainer.
- Estimated Value: $1,200 — comparable to 8–10 hours of dedicated strategist and content-planning time at typical Melbourne agency rates.
- Price: $297 — one-time, purchasable online without a sales call.
- Rationale: Passes the Rung 1 counter-test: no procurement, no internal business case needed, so the buyer can act on her own authority while she builds the case for something bigger. Exploits a real gap — every competitor in the supplied set (Print Video Web from $700/mo, SEO First Page from $490/mo) requires a recurring commitment even at entry level; none sells a single fixed-price fix.
- Ascends To: Rung 2 — the monthly Foundations Plan.
- Delivery Load: Low — templated audit-to-calendar process executed by an existing coordinator against a standard framework; no new hires required.

## RUNG 2 — MID-RANGE

**3. Service (Managed Plan): "How Marketing Managers Build A 90-Day Content Engine Without Adding Headcount"**
- Description: A fixed-scope monthly plan covering strategy, content production, scheduling and community management across two priority platforms, delivered as a documented system — shared calendar, brand-voice guide, single approval flow — that keeps running even if the internal team is short-staffed.
- Solves: "Every time we lose the person who does social, we start again" and "I don't think we've got the bandwidth to do social properly in-house anymore" — named directly in the ICP's Current Reality.
- Estimated Value: $3,000/mo — proxy replacement cost of a mid-level in-house social coordinator (salary, on-costs, software, recruitment risk) for a fraction of the multi-discipline skillset this plan provides.
- Price: $1,950/mo — no procurement process required, single decision-maker approval typical at this spend band.
- Rationale: Sits below the comparable Melbourne "Growth" package price point ($2,000/mo per the competitor analysis) while delivering a multi-discipline team instead of a generalist, giving buyers a self-fundable option that doesn't need director sign-off — directly answering ICP objection #2 ("I'm not sure the directors will approve external spend").
- Ascends To: Rung 3 — the Full Social Growth Plan.
- Delivery Load: Medium — occupies a dedicated account slot; must be capped in monthly volume against the current two-person delivery team to avoid recreating the exact capacity strain this offer is sold to solve.

## RUNG 3 — HIGH-TICKET

**4. Service (Retained Engagement): "The Full-Service Social Media Plan That Replaces Five Freelancers With One Accountable Team"**
- Description: A fully embedded monthly retainer combining organic strategy, content production, paid social campaign management and monthly board-ready reporting, run by one accountable team with a single point of contact — closed via a scoped consultation and written proposal.
- Solves: The fear of losing a client over social specifically, and the need — stated explicitly in the ICP's Goals — to present the agency's (or department's) social capability with confidence rather than downplaying it in front of directors or prospects.
- Estimated Value: $18,000/mo — proxy cost of assembling an equivalent in-house pod (strategist, designer, copywriter, paid media manager, analyst) at market salaries plus tooling.
- Price: $8,500/mo — closed via consultation, scoped in writing, minimum term applies.
- Rationale: This is the rung that genuinely requires a conversation, matching how the ICP actually buys — she must build a case for two directors. The written scope-and-investment summary delivered after the call is the exact internal-business-case toolkit the audit flagged as missing, directly removing her stated barrier: "I haven't yet built a confident business case to bring to the directors."
- Ascends To: Rung 4 — the Embedded Social Partner Program.
- Delivery Load: High — requires a dedicated cross-functional pod; number of concurrent Rung 3 clients is capped by current headcount and gated by the consultation step to protect delivery quality.

## RUNG 4 — PREMIUM / ULTRA-HIGH

**5. Asset/IP (White-Label Rights): "The White-Label Social Team Agencies Use To Stop Losing Clients Over Inconsistent Delivery"**
- Description: A fully embedded, white-label social delivery team plugged into an agency's existing account structure — strategy, content, paid and reporting across the client portfolio, delivered under the partner agency's own brand with confidentiality terms agreed up front. Capacity-limited to a small number of agency partners at any time.
- Solves: The ICP's core structural problem, named verbatim: "every time someone leaves, we're back to square one," account-manager overflow eroding margin, and the unspoken fear that outsourcing exposes a capability gap in front of clients — addressed directly by white-label, behind-the-scenes delivery.
- Estimated Value: $60,000/mo-equivalent — proxy cost of building and retaining an in-house specialist pod (strategist, two content producers, paid specialist, analyst) across a multi-account portfolio, plus the avoided cost of the repeated hiring/turnover cycle the ICP describes.
- Price: $28,500/mo — scoped and priced per portfolio size following a paid discovery consultation; minimum term applies.
- Rationale: A Rung 4 buyer is purchasing access, scarcity and risk transfer, not content. Capacity is genuinely limited by the current two-person-plus-account-manager delivery model — this is a real constraint, not manufactured scarcity, which keeps it compliant with Tier 0's no-fake-scarcity rule while still functioning as an honest exclusivity signal.
- Ascends To: Top of ladder — no further rung. Existing Rung 3 clients whose account volume grows into multi-brand or agency-scale territory are the natural resell path into this offer.
- Delivery Load: High — explicitly limited to 2–3 concurrent partner engagements at current staffing; scaling further slots requires a hiring plan before selling additional capacity.
