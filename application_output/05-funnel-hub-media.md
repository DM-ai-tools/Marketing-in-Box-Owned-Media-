# PHASE 1 — MEDIA FIT, TOPIC MAPPING & PURCHASE-INTENT RANKING

## 1. The 18 Funnel Types, Grouped by Media Fit

No canonical 18-funnel list was supplied in the inputs, so the set below was assembled to be exhaustive of the standard funnel archetypes *and* fully groundable in TrafficRadius's actual reference material (ICP, pillar page, existing funnel doc). Every funnel maps to something real, planned, or logically extendable from those documents — none are generic filler.

| # | Funnel Type | Media Fit | Reasoning (economics / format / traffic-source need) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead Magnet Funnel | **Both** | Gated guides rank organically over time *and* convert well as paid-ad landing pages; the economics don't require either channel, they require a strong gap-and-promise headline (per the ICP's own recommendation for this exact asset). |
| 2 | Content/SEO Blog Funnel | **Organic** | The entire mechanic is search-intent capture and internal-link equity (the pillar's 6 cluster pages exist for this reason). Paid spend against blog content has poor unit economics — you're paying to send traffic to a page built to earn traffic for free. |
| 3 | Webinar Funnel (Live) | **Both** | Needs a hard registration deadline to justify the "live" format, which paid ads reliably supply on a schedule — but LinkedIn organic (Rachel's stated primary channel, ICP §11) can fill a cohort just as well among agency-ops peer networks. |
| 4 | Evergreen Webinar Funnel | **Paid** | Automated/"just-in-time" webinars have no natural organic discovery path (nobody searches for a specific on-demand training); the format only works with continuous paid traffic feeding the automation. |
| 5 | Free Tool/Audit Funnel | **Both** | Already proven organically as the pillar's hero widget *and* independently flagged as ideal for dedicated paid landing pages (Design Tokens/Existing Funnel Doc both note it as "self-selecting, low friction") — the mechanic is channel-agnostic by design. |
| 6 | Quiz/Assessment Funnel | **Both** | Diagnostic quizzes are inherently shareable (organic/LinkedIn) and perform as strong-CTR paid ad creative — the format's psychology (self-relevance) works identically cold or warm. |
| 7 | Challenge Funnel | **Both** | Can be filled from an existing organic community/email base at low cost, but scaling a cohort to a meaningful size on a deadline typically needs paid amplification — genuinely dual-channel by necessity. |
| 8 | Case Study/VSL Funnel | **Both** | Long-form proof content works as an organic nurture asset (linked from emails/blog) and as a paid retargeting/cold-traffic closer — the economics depend on where in the journey it's placed, not the format itself. |
| 9 | Application Funnel | **Both** | High-intent applications are typically the *result* of prior nurture (organic) but are also commonly the direct landing target of highly-targeted paid campaigns (e.g., LinkedIn ads to "Head of Client Services") — works either way once intent is established. |
| 10 | Consultation/Strategy Call Funnel | **Both** | This is the convergence point for the entire funnel (per the Existing Funnel Doc) — by definition it must accept traffic from every channel equally; it has no channel-specific mechanic of its own. |
| 11 | Community/Membership Funnel | **Organic** | Communities are trust-and-belonging mechanics; paid ads driving cold traffic into "join our free community" convert poorly and cheapen the positioning. The ICP explicitly names peer networks and LinkedIn groups — organic, relationship-driven channels — as the *only* channels Rachel actually uses for this kind of discovery. |
| 12 | Retargeting Funnel | **Paid** | By definition a pixel/audience-based mechanic — there is no organic equivalent to "show an ad to someone who already visited." |
| 13 | Direct Response Ad Funnel | **Paid** | Cold-traffic acquisition via paid placement is the entire mechanic; the funnel doesn't exist without ad spend. |
| 14 | Summit Funnel | **Both** | Co-promotion with partner speakers/sponsors generates organic reach (each speaker promotes to their own list), while registration volume at scale typically still needs paid amplification to hit a worthwhile attendee count. |
| 15 | Tripwire/Low-Ticket Offer Funnel | **Paid** | The classic tripwire economic model *is* "the low-ticket sale funds the ad spend that acquired the customer" — it is structurally a paid-traffic funnel; running it on organic traffic wastes the mechanic's core purpose (ad-spend recovery). |
| 16 | Free Trial/Pilot Project Funnel | **Both** | Pilots are typically offered to already-warm, already-nurtured prospects (organic funnel graduates) but can equally be the direct offer behind a highly qualified paid campaign targeting agency directors. |
| 17 | Referral/Partner Funnel | **Organic** | Referral economics run entirely on existing trust and relationship capital — paid spend has no role in a referral mechanic; it is the lowest-CAC channel precisely because it isn't a media channel at all. |
| 18 | Demo/Sample Deliverable Funnel | **Both** | Works as an organic nurture step (linked from emails, per the ICP's stated desire to see "what quality looks like" before committing) and equally as a paid landing page for prospects who want proof before any conversation. |
