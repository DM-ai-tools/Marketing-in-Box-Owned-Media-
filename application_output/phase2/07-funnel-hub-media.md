# PHASE 1 — MEDIA FIT, TOPIC MAPPING & PURCHASE-INTENT RANKING

## 1.1 Media-Fit Grouping (with reasoning)

| Funnel | Group | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lead Magnet | **BOTH** | The Pricing Guide already lives on organic pillar/child-page CTAs *and* is designed for paid retargeting pools per the existing Funnel Document (Part 1B) — the economics work at any traffic cost because the ask (an email) is cheap to fulfil either way. |
| 2. Tripwire | **PAID** | A self-liquidating offer only works at volume; the math (small margin × many buyers = offset ad spend) requires predictable, scalable cold traffic that only paid can deliver on demand. |
| 3. Live Webinar | **BOTH** | Registrations can be organically sourced (existing list, LinkedIn organic per ICP §11) or paid-acquired; the format doesn't favour one traffic source over the other. |
| 4. Automated Webinar | **PAID** | Evergreen webinars exist specifically to be fed continuously by cold paid traffic at scale; without paid spend, an "evergreen" webinar has no engine driving fresh registrants. |
| 5. VSL | **PAID** | VSLs are a classic cold-traffic, direct-response mechanic — built to convert someone who has never heard of you inside one sitting; this is a paid-ads format by design. |
| 6. Application Funnel | **BOTH** | Applications can be reached via organic search intent ("apply Meta Ads agency") or via paid retargeting of warmed leads from Clusters A–D; the qualifying mechanic works regardless of source. |
| 7. Quiz/Assessment | **ORGANIC** | Interactive tools are inherently shareable and SEO-durable (the Health Check quiz is already positioned in the reference materials as a self-serve, no-waiting differentiator versus competitors' gated PDFs) — its economics reward organic distribution and backlinks, not ad spend. |
| 8. Survey | **ORGANIC** | The "Melbourne SME Meta Ads Benchmark Report" (already named as the signature asset in the Content Marketing Strategy, Section 6.1) is explicitly a PR/backlink acquisition asset — its value compounds through organic citation, not paid reach. |
| 9. Challenge | **BOTH** | A 5-day email challenge can be organically opted into via existing list/content, or paid-acquired as a lower-friction alternative to a webinar; works either way. |
| 10. Summit | **ORGANIC** | Summits succeed by borrowing co-speakers' organic audiences and email lists — the entire mechanic is designed to avoid paid acquisition cost by trading exposure between partners. |
| 11. Book | **PAID** | The free-plus-shipping mechanic (Brunson-style) exists specifically to convert cold paid traffic; almost nobody organically searches for a free physical book, but cold ad traffic responds well to a tangible, low-friction "free" offer. |
| 12. Product/Service Launch | **BOTH** | A launch (e.g. the White-Label Meta Ads Partner Program) is announced to the existing organic list/content audience and can simultaneously be paid-amplified during the cart-open window; both channels serve the urgency mechanic. |
| 13. Bridge | **PAID** | Bridge pages exist purely to pre-sell traffic arriving from a paid partner/affiliate placement before redirecting — there is no organic equivalent of "traffic referred by someone else's paid ad." |
| 14. Consultation/Strategy Call | **BOTH** | Already the terminal CTA for organic content (pillar, cluster articles) and paid retargeting alike per the existing Funnel Document — the booking mechanic itself doesn't care where the click came from. |
| 15. Free Trial/Pilot | **BOTH** | Pilots can be offered to organically-nurtured leads (post-guide, post-quiz) or paid-acquired prospects who want a lower-risk entry than a full retainer; works for either. |
| 16. Membership/Continuity | **ORGANIC** | Low-dollar recurring content products depend on low CAC to be profitable; that favours an already-warmed organic audience (newsletter subscribers, guide downloaders) over paid acquisition, which would rarely pay back at $19/month. |
| 17. Case Study/Social Proof | **ORGANIC** | These are SEO-durable, decision-stage content assets (per Cluster C/D of the Content Marketing Strategy) designed to be found via search and shared internally by a champion like Rachel — not typically the subject of standalone paid campaigns. |
| 18. Referral/Ambassador | **ORGANIC** | By definition driven by an existing client relationship and word-of-mouth; introducing ad spend into a referral mechanic would defeat its entire cost advantage. |

**Group totals:** Organic = 6 (7, 8, 10, 16, 17, 18) · Paid = 5 (2, 4, 5, 11, 13) · Both = 7 (1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 14, 15)

## 3.1 Commitment Ordering (lowest → highest ask) with reasoning

| Rung | Funnel | What's actually being asked | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead Magnet | An email address | The lowest possible commitment in the taxonomy — a single click and one field. |
| 2 | Survey | A few minutes answering questions + an email for the reward | Trivially more than pure email capture; the "cost" is a small amount of attention, not disclosure. |
| 3 | Quiz/Assessment | 3–4 minutes of interactive effort + email to unlock the full result | A step up because the visitor discloses information *about their own account*, not just contact details. |
| 4 | Case Study/Social Proof | Reading/evaluation time, usually no email required | Tiny ask on paper, but requires more sustained decision-stage attention than a quiz. |
| 5 | Referral (entry side) | A single click, carrying an implicit social commitment | Low mechanical ask, but the visitor is engaging because someone they trust vouched for it — a different kind of "cost." |
| 6 | Bridge | 2–3 minutes of sustained video attention before redirect | Requires focus rather than a single action. |
| 7 | Automated Webinar | Email + 30–60 minutes of on-demand viewing | Real time cost, but flexible/self-paced. |
| 8 | VSL | 10–20 minutes of focused attention on an overt sales pitch | Higher per-minute cost than a webinar because the viewer knows it's a pitch and chooses to stay anyway. |
| 9 | Challenge | Email + repeated daily engagement across 5 days | Incremental but real time investment, compounding day over day. |
| 10 | Live Webinar | A scheduled, calendar-blocked live attendance | Harder ask than any on-demand format — it requires showing up at a fixed time. |
| 11 | Summit | Registration + real time across a multi-day, multi-session event | Bigger total time ask than a single webinar, though attendance is often partial. |
| 12 | Book | Real money (shipping) + mailing address + payment details | The first rung where money physically changes hands. |
| 13 | Membership/Continuity | A small but *recurring* dollar commitment | Individually cheap, but an ongoing financial commitment ranks above a one-off shipping charge. |
| 14 | Tripwire | A real, one-off dollar payment for a genuine deliverable | A bigger single payment than continuity's small recurring fee, and a stronger buying signal. |
| 15 | Application | Detailed business/personal information + time + willingness to be screened or rejected | A significant psychological and time commitment before money is even discussed. |
| 16 | Free Trial/Pilot | Real (reduced) money + account access (ad account, pixel, brand assets) + onboarding time | Combines financial and operational exposure — the business is letting a vendor inside its systems. |
| 17 | Consultation/Strategy Call | A scheduled live conversation requiring the prospect to explain and defend their situation | The highest non-monetary commitment — real time plus genuine vulnerability in front of a salesperson. |
| 18 | Product/Service Launch (full retainer signup) | Recurring retainer-scale money + a contractual term + full account access + internal sign-off | The maximum total commitment of all 18 — money, time, access and organisational buy-in combined. |
