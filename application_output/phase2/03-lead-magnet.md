# PART 1 — COMPETITOR LEAD MAGNET LANDSCAPE

| Domain | Lead Magnet Name | Format | Mechanic | Gated? | Perceived Value / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kingkong.co | "Meta Ads Playbook" | Report/ebook | Name + email → instant emailed PDF | Gated | Broad authority claim (billions in sales, 1,067+ niches); static document, no personalisation |
| saar.com.au | Facebook Advertising Guide | Ebook | Short form fill → resource delivered | Gated | Generic educational guide sitting beside the service page; no stated value or personalisation |
| prospectpulse.com.au | "90-Day Blueprint" | Free audit | Director-level manual audit tied to Meta Ads page; adjacent Google Ads page quantifies an equivalent audit at $1,000 | Gated (implied consultation) | Highest stated value of the four, but requires waiting on a human-delivered audit — not instant |
| impressive.com.au | "Free Paid Audit" | Free audit | Manual review of current Meta account performance; separate "Free Discovery Call" CTA | Gated (contact form) | No dollar value stated, no downloadable asset — closest to a plain contact-form CTA |

**Synthesis (whitespace gap):** Every competitor lead magnet in this set falls into one of two buckets: a **static gated document** (King Kong, SAAR) that reads the same for every visitor and requires no interaction beyond an email address, or a **manually-delivered audit** (Prospect Pulse, Impressive) that promises value but makes the visitor wait for a human to produce it. None of the four give the visitor an **instant, self-serve, personalised result** at the point of opt-in. That's the gap: a problem-aware buyer like TrafficRadius's ICP — who is privately trying to work out whether her (or her client's) Meta Ads setup is actually sound, before she's ready to talk to anyone — has no way in this competitive set to get a real, specific answer about *her own account* without either reading generic content or booking a call. An interactive diagnostic that scores the visitor's actual current setup and returns a personalised result in minutes, with no waiting and no sales conversation required, is unclaimed territory in this exact competitive set — and it beats the "free audit" pattern on speed and beats the "gated ebook" pattern on relevance.

# PART 3 — WINNING LEAD MAGNET BRIEF

**Format:** Information — interactive diagnostic / scorecard

**Title:** "Is Your Meta Ads Spend Actually Working? Get Your Free Account Health Score"

**One-line hook:** You can report reach, clicks and impressions. What you can't say for certain — until now — is whether your Meta Ads spend is actually landing.

**Mechanic:** A 10-question interactive assessment, answered in-browser with no sign-up required to start. Each question scores one of five areas that determine whether Meta Ads spend is working or leaking: tracking & attribution, creative testing, retargeting structure, reporting clarity, and spend confidence. On completion, the visitor sees their overall Health Score (0–100) and band instantly, free, with no email required. To unlock the full category-by-category breakdown and the specific gaps behind their score, they provide a first name and work email.

**Solves:** The exact pain named on the pillar page's "Sound Familiar?" section and echoed throughout the ICP document — *"I can't prove what social returned"* and *"We're spending on ads and I'm not confident it's landing."* It also surfaces the continuity risk named directly in the ICP ("every time someone leaves, we're back to square one") via the account-ownership question in the Spend Efficiency category — letting a reader like Rachel run this privately against her own account, or a client's, before she's ready to say out loud that she needs outside help.

**Gate:** First name + work email only, and only to unlock the *detailed* breakdown — the headline score and band are shown free, which keeps the gate as light as the format allows and matches the "no obligation" framing already established sitewide.

**Estimated Value:** $150 — equivalent to roughly one hour of a strategist's time doing a manual, single-point account review; disclosed on the results screen as an estimate, not a guarantee.

**Consumption Time:** 3–4 minutes.

**Ascends To:** The existing CTA ladder, unchanged — mid-rung "Get a free Meta Ads audit" (for a deeper, human-reviewed version of the same categories) and primary "Book a free consultation call," linking to the already-built `/meta-ads-strategy-call/` booking page from the Funnel Document, where the visitor's score becomes the starting point of the conversation. It does not duplicate the pricing guide (a different pain — cost benchmarking vs. performance confidence) or the pillar's manual audit widget (a different mechanic — instant self-serve score vs. submit-and-wait).

**Compliance:** N/A — Regulated Field Flag = No. Standard Tier 0 discipline applied throughout: no guarantees, no fabricated statistics, all value estimates explicitly labelled as estimates, results framed as directional ("this suggests…") rather than diagnostic fact.

### Build notes

1. **No invented statistics.** The only numeric value on the page ($150 estimated value) is explicitly labelled an estimate, matching the framing convention already used elsewhere in this project's Rung 0 asset ($450 audit estimate). All scoring logic is transparent, deterministic arithmetic — nothing is dressed up as a "result" that wasn't actually computed from the visitor's own answers.
2. **Gate is deliberately lighter than the pricing guide's.** The pricing guide (already live in the Funnel Document) asks for name, email, role and company. This tool shows the headline score for free and only gates the detailed breakdown behind name + email — appropriate for an interactive format where friction kills completion, and intentionally differentiated from the existing asset's gate.
3. **Ascension paths are real, not invented.** Both CTAs link to pages that already exist in this project's own documents: `/meta-ads-strategy-call/` (built in Part 7 of the Funnel Document) and the pillar page's audit anchor.
4. **One placeholder retained honestly:** the `[PLACEHOLDER — insert a named, dated client example]` note in the results section, consistent with this project's established practice of flagging rather than fabricating proof.
5. **Tokens match exactly.** Every colour, font, radius and button style is pulled directly from the Brand Design Reference already established for the `/meta-ads/` page — nothing new was introduced.
