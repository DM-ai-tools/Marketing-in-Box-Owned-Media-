**Whitespace synthesis (per Step 1):**

Every single benchmarked competitor — 8 of 8 — runs the identical mechanic: a **free SEO audit**, direct-to-brand, promising a diagnostic before a sales conversation. There is zero format diversity in this competitive set: no checklist, no template, no calculator, no quiz, no community, no downloadable document of any kind. The one attempt at differentiation (23 Digital's proprietary tracking tool) is post-sale, not the actual gate. Two structural gaps stand out. First, **format**: "free audit" is now table stakes in Melbourne digital marketing, to the point that TrafficRadius's own live hero-widget audit is already doing exactly what every competitor does — building a second audit variant would add TrafficRadius to an 9-for-9 saturated field rather than differentiate it. Second, and more decisive: **audience**. All eight competitors, without exception, speak exclusively to brands managing their own marketing — none addresses the agency-to-agency buyer at all. This is precisely the blind spot the ICP document names directly: Rachel Nguyen's research is hampered because "most content she encounters is aimed at brands managing their own social, not agencies needing overflow or specialist support." No competitor in this market currently occupies that space in any lead-magnet format. That combination — an unclaimed *format* (document/checklist) serving an unclaimed *audience* (agency ops leads) — is the single biggest whitespace gap available.

# PART 3 — Winning Lead Magnet Brief

**Format:** Information — Checklist (document)

**Title:** "The Agency Social Delivery Checklist: Stop Losing Client Trust Every Time Someone Leaves"

**One-line hook:** *"Every time we lose the person who does social, we're back to square one" — here's what to document so a resignation costs you a few awkward weeks, not a full reset.*

**Mechanic:** A single-page, self-contained checklist covering the four places agency social delivery actually breaks — documentation & knowledge transfer, workload & capacity visibility, client-facing consistency, and redundancy planning. Visitors complete a short form to unlock the full checklist in-page; interactive checkboxes let them self-score their current exposure and receive a plain-language read on where they stand, with no numeric claim attached to it.

**Solves:** Directly targets the ICP's named structural fear — *"every time someone leaves, we're back to square one"* — and the related commercial exposure she hasn't yet articulated upward: account-manager overflow eroding margin, no documented handover process, and the belief that "just hire another coordinator" will repeat the same 12–18 month cycle.

**Gate:** Name, work email, agency name, role (required) + agency size, current situation (optional) — reused exactly from the already-approved Page 2B field set in the Funnel Document, so this asset slots into the existing Agency Delivery Desk mechanic rather than creating a competing one.

**Estimated Value:** $450 — equivalent to roughly 3 hours of a senior agency-ops consultant's time to build an equivalent audit-and-documentation framework internally.

**Consumption Time:** ~12 minutes to read and self-score.

**Ascends To:** The Agency Delivery Desk community (Page 3B — Community Welcome), which itself ladders to **Book a Free Consultation** — both real, already-defined steps in the Funnel Document and Offers Ladder (Rung 1 → community → Rung 2/3 consultation).

**Compliance:** Not applicable — Regulated Field Flag is No, and no diagnosis, guarantee, or regulated claim is made anywhere in the asset; self-scoring bands are qualitative only, with no numeric outcome promised.
