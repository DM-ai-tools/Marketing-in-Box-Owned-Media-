## 1. Data Collection Summary — Read This Before the Rest of the Report

**Collection method:** Live browser research, no login, no social-listening tool connected in this session.

**Critical limitation, disclosed upfront:** Every platform in scope actively blocks post-history access to unauthenticated visitors:
- **LinkedIn** shows a hard authwall on every company page's Posts tab — zero post data obtainable for any of the 6 accounts.
- **Instagram** shows the bio, follower/following counts, and a thumbnail grid, but no caption text is exposed in the accessible page content without logging in — topics/purpose cannot be classified from what's visible.
- **Facebook** shows only the single most recent post (occasionally with reactions/comments) before prompting a login wall — not a 30-day history.
- **YouTube** is the only channel where a real post (video) list loaded without login.

This means the sample size for every account below is **far smaller than the "371 posts" scale of the reference audit** — realistically 0-1 visible items per platform per account. I have not fabricated additional posts or invented plausible-sounding percentages to fill this gap. ## 4. Consolidated Gap Analysis: Traffic Radius's Own Page

Given the data-access limitation, this section leans on what's genuinely verified (Section 2-3) plus the ICP/CRO/competitor context already established earlier in this engagement, rather than inventing false precision:

1. **Social Media Marketing is not visibly named in Traffic Radius's own Instagram bio**, despite being a core locked service pillar — the bio leads with SEO/PPC/Paid Social/Web Development. Evidence: Section 2.1. Fix: update the bio to name Social Media Marketing explicitly.
2. **YouTube channel is functionally dormant** (106 videos banked, nothing recent) while First Page, SIXGUN, and SOUP all show active Facebook posting in the same window. Evidence: Section 2.1 vs 2.2/2.4/2.5.
3. **No visible named-individual voice** in Traffic Radius's own recent content, while SOUP Agency's most recent visible post is explicitly attributed to a named Content Strategy Manager. Evidence: Section 2.1 vs 2.5. This connects directly to the Partner Voice mechanic already scoped in the LinkedIn Marketing & Ads sub-service brief from the Plan of Action — it is not being visibly practised on the brand's own channels yet.
4. **No visible founder/leadership engagement** on the brand's own posts, while SIXGUN's founder personally commented on their most recent visible post. Evidence: Section 2.4.
5. **Whitespace confirmed, not just asserted:** none of the 5 competitors' single visible post treated Social Media Marketing as its own topic — reinforcing (with fresh, if thin, evidence) the same gap this engagement's earlier competitor research already found.

## 5. Recommended Content Strategy

- **Bio/profile fix (immediate, zero-cost):** Add "Social Media Marketing" explicitly to the Instagram bio and any other profile copy that currently omits it.
- **Reactivate YouTube on a modest, sustainable cadence** (1 short video per month) rather than leaving 106 videos as a dead asset — the Four Causes Scorecard walkthrough video already scoped in the Content Marketing Strategy is a ready-made first upload.
- **Adopt the named-individual voice on owned channels**, mirroring what SOUP Agency is visibly doing and what Traffic Radius's own CRO framework and LinkedIn brief already call for: have a named strategist or the founder post/comment under their own name at least monthly.
- **Target purpose mix (directional, not derived from competitor data):** given the ICP's stated preference for diagnostic, non-salesy content, weight owned social toward Education and Awareness purposes (per the existing CRO framework), with Culture/Retention content used sparingly and always tied to a real, specific moment (not generic team photos).
- **Platform strategic roles:**
  - *Facebook* — proof-of-activity layer; keep posting at least fortnightly so the brand doesn't read as dormant the way nimbl's does.
  - *Instagram* — visual case-study and Four Causes Scorecard promotion.
  - *LinkedIn* — primary channel for the named-individual/Partner Voice mechanic (matches the ICP's stated primary research surface).
  - *YouTube* — long-form diagnostic and process-walkthrough home, reactivated on a monthly cadence.
