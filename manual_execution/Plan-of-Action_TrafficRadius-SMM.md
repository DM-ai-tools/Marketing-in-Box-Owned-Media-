## 1. Executive Summary

This plan takes Traffic Radius's Social Media Marketing pillar from a single completed pillar-page ecosystem (Phase 1, already delivered) to two dedicated sub-service ecosystems — Meta Ads Management and LinkedIn Marketing & Ads — in Phase 2, then a full topical-authority build-out across 7 industry verticals, 8 Australian city pages, 18 additional blog posts, 6 case studies, 6 video scripts, 4 more lead magnets, and newsletter infrastructure in Phase 3. In total the plan specifies **71 additional/confirmed assets and 6 conversion funnels** across an 8-month build (compressed from a standard 12 months because Phase 1 is already complete and Phase 2 covers only the two sub-services requested). Headline opportunity: an estimated **~4,800/mo combined AU search volume** across the Phase 2 + Phase 3 keyword clusters below (all volumes are judgement-based estimates — no keyword-volume tool export was supplied, so none of these figures should be treated as verified research).

**Assumed monthly asset velocity:** Delivery Capacity is stated as Full Agency — this plan assumes 8–10 assets shipped per month, consistent with running two production tracks (content + technical/HTML build) in parallel.
