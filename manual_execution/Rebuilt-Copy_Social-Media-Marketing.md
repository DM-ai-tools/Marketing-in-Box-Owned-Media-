# 3. PROBLEM → SOLUTION
### Layer: Oxytocin
**This section does not currently exist. It is the single highest-impact addition to the page.**

**H2:** You're posting. You're boosting. You still can't tell what's working.

**Body:**
Here's what most Australian business owners tell us on the first call.

**The four pains — as cards:**

**"We post consistently and nothing happens."**
Content without a strategy is a hobby. Reach without intent is noise. If nobody has mapped which platform your buyers actually use and what makes them act, consistency just makes you consistently invisible.

**"We boost posts sometimes but we've no idea if it works."**
Boosted posts are the most expensive way to buy the least useful traffic. Without a pixel, conversion events and proper campaign structure, you're paying Meta to guess.

**"Our agency sends us a report we don't understand."**
Impressions up. Engagement rate up. Revenue flat. If a report doesn't end in leads, bookings or sales, it isn't a report — it's a receipt.

**"We can't prove social media pays for itself."**
So it's the first budget cut when things get tight. Not because it doesn't work, but because nobody set it up to be measurable.

**Turn line:**
Every one of those is a tracking and strategy problem, not a content problem. That's where we start.

**H3:** What we do instead

Three sentences, in this order:

1. **We find out where your buyers actually are** — and stop spending on the platforms where they aren't.
2. **We build the measurement first** — pixel, conversion events, GA4, server-side tagging where it's needed — so that from day one every lead traces back to a campaign.
3. **We report in your language** — leads, bookings, calls, sales, cost per each. Vanity metrics stay in the appendix.

**CTA:** Book a Free Strategy Call
