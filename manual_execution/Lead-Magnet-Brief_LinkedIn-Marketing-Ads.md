## PART 3 — WINNING LEAD MAGNET BRIEF

**Format:** Diagnostic / Interactive Scorecard

**Title:** *The Partner Voice Audit* — "How Much of Your LinkedIn Presence Actually Comes From a Person?"

**One-line hook:** You've probably already seen it — a partner's rough, unscripted post outperforming your whole content calendar. This tells you exactly how far into that pattern your firm actually is.

**Mechanic:** A 10-question interactive assessment. Each answer is scored 0–3 (0 = most company-led, 3 = most partner-led), summed to a 0–30 raw score and converted to a 0–100 Partner Voice Score, revealed live in the browser immediately after the final question — no server round-trip required. The score lands in one of three bands (Company-Led / Mixed Signal / Partner-Led), and the full breakdown — which specific answers pulled the score down, what's already working, and a benchmark comparison — unlocks after a two-field email gate.

**Solves:** Directly targets the ICP's most specific, already-lived symptom (ICP §2, current-reality section, and stated verbatim at §3: *"Michael posts something off the cuff from an airport and gets more traction than our whole content calendar. What does that tell me?"*). This tool converts that lived-but-unmeasured pattern into a specific number and a ranked list of what to fix first.

**Gate:** First name + work email only, requested after the score band is teased but before the detailed breakdown is revealed — identical friction level and identical gate placement logic to the Four Causes Scorecard, for consistency across the two tools and because that mechanic is already proven in this engagement rather than being an unvalidated new choice.

**Estimated Value:** $150 — comparable to a one-hour discovery call at typical agency rates, consistent with the same valuation basis already used for the Four Causes Scorecard.

**Consumption Time:** Under 3 minutes (10 multiple-choice questions, roughly 15–20 seconds each) — comfortably under the 20-minute Rung 0 ceiling, and shorter than the Four Causes Scorecard's own ~5 minutes, appropriate for a narrower, single-mechanism tool.

**Ascends To:** The already-drafted **LinkedIn Strategy Session** booking page (`/book-linkedin-strategy-session/`) as the primary next step, and the **Social Media Marketing Strategy Consultation** as the secondary, sitewide next step for visitors whose result suggests a broader issue than LinkedIn alone — both real, already-defined destinations, no new funnel stage invented.

**Compliance:** Not applicable — Regulated Field Flag is No.
