**Synthesis (whitespace analysis):**

The free-consultation-as-lead-magnet is saturated — three competitors (BAMBRICK, Digital Next, Web Marketing Experts) lead with an undifferentiated "book a call" mechanic, which TrafficRadius already has covered via its own live Strategy Consultation and doesn't need a fourth entrant into. Gated static documents (ebook, checklist) appear twice (Social Status, Missing Link) and are executed at a reasonable standard, meaning a new document-format magnet would need genuine content differentiation to compete, not just format novelty. Two competitors (Social Status, Attention Experts) lean on results guarantees as their primary hook — a pattern TrafficRadius's own compliance-aware, no-fake-guarantee positioning should not imitate.

**The single biggest whitespace gap: not one of the ten competitors offers any interactive, self-scoring tool** — no quiz, no calculator, no diagnostic instrument of any kind. Every lead magnet in this set is either a static document, a video/webinar, an event, or a human-delivered call. For an ICP whose stated blocker is diagnostic ambiguity rather than a lack of information ("I can't name the problem precisely enough"), an instant, self-serve diagnostic tool directly answers the exact question she's stuck on — in a format zero competitors currently offer.

**Scoring notes:**
- **B** loses on Differentiation — gated guides are already a proven, moderately saturated format in this set (2 competitors), and content novelty alone doesn't claim new territory.
- **C** loses on Deliverability — a benchmark report needs real, periodically refreshed data to avoid the exact staleness problem already flagged elsewhere in this engagement (a competitor's own 2023-dated report was found still live in 2026). Worth building later, not first.
- **D** scores well and is a strong second choice — narrower in scope (one specific blind spot: the CAC honesty gap) versus A's full diagnostic coverage across all four causes.
- **A wins** on the combination that matters most here: it is the only candidate that directly answers Dani's single largest named barrier, claims a completely unclaimed format in the competitive set, and was already partially scoped in the Value Ladder document — meaning less net-new design risk to build correctly.

**Winner: A — The Four Causes Scorecard**

## PART 3 — WINNING LEAD MAGNET BRIEF

**Format:** Diagnostic / Interactive Scorecard

**Title:** *The Four Causes Scorecard* — "Find Out Which of the Four Causes Is Behind Your Flat Social Media Performance"

**One-line hook:** You know social isn't working. This tells you *why*, in under five minutes — before you talk to anyone.

**Mechanic:** A 12-question interactive assessment (3 questions per cause: channel-fit, brand-architecture, operating-model, measurement). Each answer is scored for severity; the tool totals the four categories live in the browser and reveals the dominant cause immediately, with a plain-language explanation of what that cause means in practice and what to do about it first.

**Solves:** Directly targets ICP §8's single largest stated barrier: *"She can't name the problem precisely enough to buy a solution... Everything she can see is a symptom."* This tool converts an unanswerable question ("what's wrong?") into a specific, evidenced answer in one sitting.

**Gate:** First name + email only, requested after the score is calculated but before the detailed breakdown and explanation are revealed — the visitor sees enough of a teaser (their dominant cause, named, with no explanation) to want the unlock, but the gate itself asks for the minimum two fields. No company name, phone, or business details requested — deliberately lower-friction than the existing Audit Request form, consistent with a true Rung 0 asset.

**Estimated Value:** $150 — comparable to a one-hour discovery call at typical agency rates.

**Consumption Time:** Under 5 minutes (12 multiple-choice questions, roughly 20–25 seconds each).

**Ascends To:** The already-live **Free Social Media Audit** (for a considered next step) and, for anyone whose total severity score is high across multiple causes, a direct prompt toward the **Social Media Marketing Strategy Consultation** — both existing funnel assets, no new destination required.

**Compliance:** Not applicable — Regulated Field Flag is No.
