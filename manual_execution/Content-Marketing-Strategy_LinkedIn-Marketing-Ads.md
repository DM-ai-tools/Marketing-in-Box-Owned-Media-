### 2.3 Identified Content Gaps (Traffic Radius Opportunities)

**Gap 1 — LinkedIn-specific service and content depth.** Confirmed absent across all ten competitors. Blog Post 1 (already written) and the Partner Voice Audit already claim this; Cluster A's remaining sub-topics extend it.

**Gap 3 — Thought Leader Ads and employee advocacy, explained for B2B/professional services specifically.** These are LinkedIn-specific paid and organic mechanics that generalist competitors, if they mention LinkedIn at all, do not explain in any depth. Cluster B exists specifically to close this, including the already-scoped Blog Post 2.

**Gap 4 — Compliance-aware LinkedIn content operations for regulated-adjacent professional services.** No competitor content addresses how a partner-led firm publishes personal, timely LinkedIn content without breaching compliance review timelines or professional standards. Cluster D's vertical deep-dives are built to close this per-industry.

**Gap 5 — Vertical-specific LinkedIn guidance within professional services.** All ten competitors, where they mention B2B or LinkedIn at all, write for a generic B2B audience. None address accounting, legal, engineering, financial advisory, or architecture firms specifically. Cluster D exists to close this.

Distinct from the main Social Media Marketing pillar's own signature asset (the Social Media Marketing Benchmark Report), to avoid cannibalising it and to give the LinkedIn cluster its own flagship.

- **Data source:** Aggregated, de-identified Partner Voice Audit completions once volume is sufficient (per the KSI table, ~150/month by Month 12) — genuinely earned data from the tool already built, not a fabricated industry statistic.

- **Timing constraint:** This asset cannot be built until the Partner Voice Audit has enough completions to aggregate responsibly (recommend Month 9–10 of this plan, not Month 1) — flagged here rather than scheduled prematurely in Section 7's calendar.

## 8. Distribution & Amplification Plan

Only channels confirmed available are included below. No PR/backlink outreach programme or podcast channel is confirmed for this client specifically, so those subsections are omitted rather than planned speculatively.

### 8.1 Organic Distribution (Always-On)

- **On-page SEO checklist per article:** primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, and one H2; meta description 150–160 characters; internal links (up to pillar, sideways to 2–3 siblings); image alt text on every image.
- **Internal linking minimum:** every cluster article carries at least 3 internal links (1 up, 2 sideways minimum) — matching the interlinking rule in Section 4.
- **Schema markup:** `Article` on every cluster piece; `FAQPage` on any article carrying an FAQ section (recommended for at least the Cluster A/C pieces answering the long-tail questions in Section 5.3).
- **Search Console monitoring cadence:** weekly, by the SEO Lead — tracking new impressions/clicks against the keyword map in Section 5, flagged in the weekly review (Section 9).

### 8.2 Email Distribution

- **Newsletter cadence:** the LinkedIn cluster gets one dedicated mention per month within the existing newsletter (not a separate LinkedIn-only send, since no dedicated LinkedIn list exists yet) — surfacing that month's strongest new article and, where relevant, the Partner Voice Audit.
- **Nurture sequence source content:** the already-built 3-email Partner Voice Funnel sequence remains the dedicated nurture path for anyone who completes the audit; the newsletter is a separate, lower-frequency top-of-funnel channel, not a duplicate of that sequence.
- **Segmentation logic:** persona (Priya-coded contacts, e.g. Practice Manager/Coordinator job titles, get the how-to/checklist-format articles surfaced first) × vertical (once Cluster D content exists, segment by industry field already captured on the audit request form).

### 8.3 LinkedIn/Social Amplification

- **Founder/CEO post cadence:** 1 post/week, timed to each week's new long-form asset — written in first person, not a repost of the article's own summary, consistent with the individual-voice argument this entire sub-service is built around.
- **Team member post cadence:** Social Lead coordinates 1–2 team reshares/comments per week on the founder's post, modelling the exact employee-advocacy behaviour Cluster B content recommends to clients.
- **Company page re-share pattern:** company page reshares the founder's post roughly 24–48 hours after the original, supporting rather than competing with the individual post — again, modelling the pillar page's own stated operating model.
- **Engagement quota:** Social Lead spends 15 minutes/day responding to comments on LinkedIn cluster-related posts — comment activity is where relationships form on LinkedIn per the pillar page's own stated view, and it's the part most competitor content ignores entirely.

### 8.4 Paid Amplification

- **Retargeting audience definition:** visitors to the LinkedIn pillar page or any Cluster A/B article who didn't complete the Partner Voice Audit, retargeted via LinkedIn Insight Tag and/or Google Ads remarketing, window 30 days.
- **Test budget:** a modest LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads test running the founder's own highest-performing organic post (per Section 8.3) to a firmographically defined audience (professional services, 20–150 employees, Melbourne/Victoria) — stated as a test budget for validation, not a guaranteed-spend commitment, consistent with the Plan of Action's own no-guarantee framing.

### 10.3 Quality Checklist (Pre-Publish)

- **Originality:** zero AI-generated boilerplate; every piece passes human editorial review before publishing, consistent with the standard already set for Blog Post 1.
- **Accuracy/sourcing:** every claim about LinkedIn's own platform behaviour (e.g. distribution mechanics) is stated as consistent with LinkedIn's publicly described product behaviour, not presented as insider or leaked information; every AUD figure is either sourced from real client/portfolio data or clearly labelled an estimate — no invented statistics, per the ACCC/Australian Consumer Law standard already applied sitewide.
- **Voice/localisation:** matches the direct, peer-level, diagnostic tone established across the pillar page, funnel, and Blog Post 1; Australian spelling and AUD currency throughout.
- **Compliance:** no absolute outcome claims; conditional language ("can," "may," "in many cases," "results vary") applied to any performance-adjacent statement, matching the standard already used in every FAQ answer across this engagement; content addressing regulated-adjacent verticals (Cluster D's law firm piece specifically) carries a general-information note, consistent with the compliance framing already established for compliance-sensitive content elsewhere in this engagement.
