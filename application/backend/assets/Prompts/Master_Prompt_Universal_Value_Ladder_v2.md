## THE VALUE LADDER MODEL

Five rungs. As price rises, four things shift in lockstep:
**ACCESS:** one-to-many → one-to-few → one-to-one
**CUSTOMISATION:** generic → templated → bespoke
**LABOUR:** DIY → done-with-you → done-for-you → done-for-you + strategy
**OUTCOME:** information → implementation → transformation → business/life change

**RUNG 0 — FREE / LEAD GENERATION**
Price: 0, paid for with an email address or phone number. Job: prove competence, capture contact,
create reciprocity. Buyer state: problem-aware, doesn't know you. Consumption time: under 20
minutes.

**RUNG 1 — LOW-TICKET (entry / tripwire)**
Job: convert a lead into a buyer. The identity shift matters more than the revenue. Buyer state:
needs no permission from anyone to buy. Instant decision. Test: could they buy it without a
conversation — on their phone, at the counter, in one click? If no, it's Rung 2.

**RUNG 2 — MID-RANGE**
Job: deliver a real, self-contained result. Funds the business. Qualifies buyers upward. Buyer
state: will compare 2–3 options, may sleep on it. Still one decision-maker. Test: no procurement
process, no sales call strictly required.

**RUNG 3 — HIGH-TICKET**
Job: solve an expensive or high-stakes problem. Requires a conversation before purchase. Buyer
state: has budget, has urgency, is buying certainty and speed. Test: needs an application, call,
quote, or proposal to close.

**RUNG 4 — PREMIUM / ULTRA-HIGH**
Job: transformation, exclusivity, or full ownership of an outcome. Buyer state: buying access,
scarcity, and risk transfer. Test: capacity-constrained — you could only serve a handful at once.

**How the rungs typically manifest by Business Model Type** (illustrative, not prescriptive):

| Rung | Practitioner | Agency / B2B | Trade / Field Service | Product / Retail | SaaS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Free guide/quiz | Free audit/scorecard | Free inspection/quote | Free sample/trial size | Freemium/free trial |
| 1 | Mini-course, template | Teardown, workshop | Small fixed-price callout | Starter kit, add-on | Entry-tier plan |
| 2 | Group program, cohort | Fixed-scope project | Standard install/service job | Core product line | Growth-tier plan |
| 3 | 1:1 coaching program | Retained engagement | Major installation/renovation | Bespoke/custom build | Enterprise contract |
| 4 | Mastermind, retreat | Fractional exec/embedded team | Full-property retrofit, VIP contract | Wholesale/private-label deal | Strategic partnership |

## PRICE CALIBRATION (industry-agnostic)

Never assume dollar amounts. Derive the ladder from the client's own economics:

1. Establish the **ANCHOR**: the annual value of the outcome to the customer (revenue gained,
   cost avoided, hours saved × their rate, risk removed) — from the Financial Anchor Inputs.
2. Set **Rung 3** at roughly 10–20% of that anchor value.
3. Derive the rest from Rung 3: Rung 0 = free (perceived value 0.5–3% of Rung 3) · Rung 1 = 1–5%
   of Rung 3 · Rung 2 = 10–25% of Rung 3 · Rung 4 = 3–10x Rung 3.
4. Sanity-check against the stated customer budget/local market norms input.
5. Price in the specified currency (default USD), using price points normal for that market and
   category (e.g. 997 / 1,200 / 15,000 — not 1,013.47).
6. **State every price exclusive of tax, and do not mention tax at all.** No "+ GST", no "inc VAT",
   no sales-tax lines, no gross-ups, and no tax caveats under the rungs. The ladder is a pricing
   architecture, not a quote: what registers with the buyer is the number and what it buys, and a
   tax notation on every rung buries that while implying a jurisdiction this document may not be
   for. Where a competitor's published price was tax-inclusive, compare against the figure as the
   competitor analysis reports it rather than adjusting it — a derived tax-exclusive equivalent
   would be an invented number.

If no anchor was supplied, state the assumption used in one line before the offer list, then
proceed. Do not stall the response waiting for a number that wasn't supplied.

## PERCEIVED VALUE RULES

- Paid offers: perceived value should be 2–5x price. Premium rungs: 1.5–3x.
- Value must be justified by a mechanism, not asserted: comparable market rate (from the
  competitor analysis if supplied), cost of the alternative, hours of expert time, or value of
  the outcome avoided/gained.
- Never invent results, testimonials, guarantees, or credentials not supplied in Team Credentials
  or Existing Offers.

## REGULATED CONSTRAINTS (apply per the resolved Claim Substantiation Tier)

- **Tier 0** — claims must be substantiated; no fake scarcity; no unsupported superlatives.
- **Tier 1** — adds: no misleading impressions by omission; any comparative claim must be
  like-for-like and dated.
- **Tier 2** — adds: no outcome guarantees; every offer requiring a licence, registration, or
  professional-indemnity cover gets a one-line `Compliance:` note; never propose an offer needing
  a credential not listed in Team Credentials.
- **Tier 3** — adds: no clinical or therapeutic outcome promises; hedge language in every Solves
  and Rationale line ("may", "can", "in many cases", "results vary", "subject to assessment");
  testimonial-style value claims only where the jurisdiction permits them.

Higher tiers inherit all constraints below them. If Claim Substantiation Tier is UNSURE, assess
the most conservative plausible tier from the stated Industry and say so in Step 0.

## HEADLINE FRAMEWORK COMPLIANCE (governs every "Title" line in the Output Schema)

A `"Title"` in this ladder is a headline, not a label. Every Title MUST be derived from
`assets/Prompts/COMPREHENSIVE_HEADLINE_FRAMEWORK.md` — the canonical headline reference for
this system. Consult it before writing a single Title; never invent headline structure from
scratch. This is a requirement, not supporting material: a Title built any other way is
non-compliant output regardless of how good it reads.

The Framework is supplied in full in the REFERENCE LIBRARY block above this prompt's INPUTS —
read it there. The numbered rules below are not a summary you may skip in favour of the file, nor a
substitute for it: they are the minimum every Title must satisfy, and the Framework governs the
detail. Its headline-formulas-by-traffic-type families, its per-channel character limits, and its
pre-publication checklist are the source of record, and all three are in the block above. Where a
rule below points at the Framework for specifics, go and get them rather than approximating.

1. **The core equation:** Interest = Curiosity + A BIG PROMISE. Every Title must both open a
   gap (something the buyer doesn't yet know) and state the strongly desired benefit — never
   one without the other.
2. **Match the rung to a traffic temperature**, then pull the matching formula family from the
   Framework's headline-formulas-by-traffic-type section:
   - **Rung 0 (Free / Lead Gen)** → Cold Traffic formulas — Problem Identification, Solution
     Introduction, Mistake Identification, Contrast/Juxtaposition, Authority Backing.
   - **Rung 1–2 (Low-Ticket / Mid-Range)** → Warm Traffic formulas — Solution Superiority,
     Specific Outcome, Proof/Social Proof, Expert Endorsement, Comparison, Objection Removal.
   - **Rung 3–4 (High-Ticket / Premium)** → Hot Traffic formulas — Guarantee, Exclusivity,
     Risk Reversal, Final Benefit Stack, Action Trigger.
3. **Incorporate at least 2 of the Framework's 8 curiosity elements** (Specificity, New
   Methodology, Credibility & Authority, Social Proof, Juxtaposition/Contrast,
   Controversy/Scandal, Action-Oriented Language, Comparison) per Title. Specificity is never
   optional — replace every vague benefit with a number, timeframe, or named mechanism.
4. **Respect the format constraints for the channel this offer will actually be promoted on**
   (the Framework's per-channel character limits and templates for ad copy, offer/promotion,
   landing page, and blog headlines) — pull the channel from "How To Deploy This Rung."
5. Run every Title through the Framework's pre-publication checklist before finalizing the
   offer list; a Title that fails Specificity or Differentiation must be rewritten, not shipped.
6. All claim-bearing headline elements (Authority, Social Proof, Guarantee, Controversy) remain
   fully subject to REGULATED CONSTRAINTS above — a curiosity-driven Title never overrides the
   resolved Claim Substantiation Tier.
