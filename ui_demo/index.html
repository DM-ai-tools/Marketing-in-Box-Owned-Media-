Part 1 — CRO Audit Report

Audit 1 — Above-the-fold clarity

Finding. The hero leads with “Melbourne’s Results-Driven Digital Marketing Agency”. That is a category claim. Nothing above the fold names the visitor, the outcome, or the timeframe, and the only CTA is “Get a Free Quote” — a request for the visitor’s effort before any value has been established.

“Melbourne’s Results-Driven Digital Marketing Agency” — existing H1

Recommendation. Lead with the outcome and a timeframe the client can substantiate. Move the category claim to the subhead where it does no harm.

Audit 2 — Proof and specificity

Finding. Five client logos sit above any claim they could support, and three testimonials carry first names only, with no result attached.

Recommendation. Pair each logo row with the one metric that client moved. Attach a number to every testimonial or cut it.
