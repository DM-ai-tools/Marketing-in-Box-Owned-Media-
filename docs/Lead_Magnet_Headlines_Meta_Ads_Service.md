# Lead Magnet Headlines — Meta Ads Service (Cross-Platform)

Target Service being promoted (constant across every platform below): **Meta Ads management**.
The platform column only changes *where* the lead magnet is promoted — the topic is never about
that platform's own advertising mechanics (e.g. Google Ads platform tips, Instagram growth tips,
LinkedIn organic tips). Every headline below pitches the same underlying offer: help with Meta
Ads performance.

Built against `docs/COMPREHENSIVE_HEADLINE_FRAMEWORK.md.pdf` — see that file for the full
psychology, per-channel format constraints, and headline-formula tables referenced below.
Funnel-stage mapping used throughout: **TOFU = Cold Traffic** (problem-aware only), **MOFU =
Warm Traffic** (comparing solutions), **BOFU = Hot Traffic** (ready to act).

Content Type legend (one tag per headline, drawn from the agreed set): `Audit Checklist` ·
`Swipe File` · `Budget Calculator` · `Targeting Blueprint` · `Proof Metrics` · `ROI Calculator` ·
`Mistake Identification` · `Frictionless Action` · `The "Next Step" Bridge`.

## Notes on what was deliberately avoided

- No headline pitches the distribution platform's own services (no "grow your Instagram,"
  "LinkedIn organic strategy," or "Google Ads tips" topics) — every single one of the 100 above
  targets the Meta Ads service regardless of where it's promoted, per the explicit instruction.
- Content Type tags were spread across all nine categories on every platform rather than
  clustering on one or two, so the ladder of lead magnets stays varied at each funnel stage.
- AEO/GEO headlines are phrased as full natural-language questions rather than compressed ad
  copy, since that phrasing is what generative/answer engines actually match against real
  queries — a direct application of the Framework's blog/search-intent guidance to this newer
  channel.
