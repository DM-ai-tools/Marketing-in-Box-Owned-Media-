Questions worth asking before switching
"What if Google Ads doesn't work for my trade?"+

Google Ads works best for exactly the buyer behaviour your trade attracts — someone with a problem right now, searching for who to call. Unlike social media ads that interrupt someone who didn't ask, search ads meet buyers at the exact moment of intent. The question is not whether it works for trades; it is whether the setup is done correctly for your specific service area and job type.

"I have tried Google Ads before and wasted money."+

That is almost always a setup problem, not a platform problem. The most common causes: broad match keywords wasting spend on irrelevant searches, no negative keyword list, campaigns sending clicks to a generic homepage, and no proper conversion tracking. All fixable. A well-structured campaign for a trade business looks nothing like a generic DIY account.

"How much do I need to spend to see results?"+

At your job value, the ROI maths work at a far lower budget than most business owners assume. Use the calculator above with your actual numbers. For a trade business with a $5,000+ average job, even $1,500 per month in ad spend can generate a positive return if the campaign is built correctly. The benchmark cost per lead for trades in Melbourne sits at $80–$140 depending on the service.

"Can I run both directories and Google Ads together?"+

Yes — and many clients start that way. The natural progression is to use Google Ads data to see what is converting, increase the budget as results come in, and then make a data-informed decision about whether the directory subscription is still justified. Most clients reduce or cancel their directory spend within 6 months of seeing their Google Ads results.
