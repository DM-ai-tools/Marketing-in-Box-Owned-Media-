04 — What the Data Shows

What Separates Top-Quartile Accounts from Average

It's rarely the budget. Across hundreds of Australian retail Meta accounts, the same four structural differences appear in top performers — regardless of category or spend level.

Factor ✗ Average Account ✓ Top Quartile Account
🎨 Creative Rotation 1–2 ads running, same creative for 8+ weeks, high frequency, declining CTR 5–8 active ad variations, fresh creative every 2–3 weeks, frequency capped at 3.0
👥 Audience Structure Single broad audience or one Lookalike, no exclusions, overlapping audiences Prospecting/retargeting separated, exclusions applied, layered Lookalikes 1–5%
🎯 Campaign Objective Traffic or Reach campaigns used for sales, optimising for clicks not purchases Sales objective throughout, pixel event correctly set to Purchase from day one
🔁 Funnel Depth Single campaign driving cold traffic to product page, no retargeting or nurture Full funnel: awareness → consideration → conversion with dedicated retargeting layer
